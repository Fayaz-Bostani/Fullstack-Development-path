About

KabulWheel is a data-driven web project designed to evaluate and rank the most popular Sport Utility Vehicles(SUVs) in the Afghan market.this platform analyzes vehicles based on key metrics including exterior design, luxury featuresm durablity in rugged terrains, and market resale value, providing a comprehensive guide for automotive enthusiasts.

Top 5 SUV Rankings

Lexus Lx-series (Lx 600)

This vehicle stands as the pinnacle of prestige in major Afghan cities. Its massive spindle grille and sharp LED signature offer an unmatched presence on the road.

inside, the LX600 features a dual-screen interface and premium semi-aniline leather, making it the most technollogically advanced SUV in the country.

Toyota Land Cruiser 300

Known as the "King of the Road," the LC300 is the most respected vehicle across all provinces due to its legendry reliability and off-road capability.

The new GR-Sport edition introduces a more aggressive aesthetic, combining ruggedness with a sophisticated interior tailored for high-ranking professionals.

Toyota 4Runner (Limited Edition)

The 4Runner is the top choice for the younger generation in Kabul, offering a muscular, American-style body that stands out from typical Asian market designs.

The limited trim adds 20-inch chrome wheels and a luxury JBL sound system, bridging the gap between a tough off-roader and a stylish city cruiser.

Toyota Prado

The prado remains a market favorite for its perfect balance of size and efficiency. it is highly valued for its low maintenance costs and its ability to navigate through Kabul's dense traffic with ease.

Hyndai Palisade

Representing the new wave of Korean luxury, the Palisade offers a futuristic design and a cobin inspired by private jets, making it a unique alternative Japanese SUVs.
