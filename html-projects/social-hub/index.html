Top Social Media Platforms

Instagram
Instagram is a poular platform for sharing photos, video, reels, and stories. it is owned by Meta and has more than 2 billions users worldwide.
YouTube
YouTube is the world's largest video-sharing platform where people watch tutorials, entertainment videos, and music. it is owned by Google and has over 2.5 billion users.
TikTok
TikTok is famous for short videos, trends, and viral content. The app became very popualr because of its smart recommendation system and currently has around 1.5 billion users.
Facebook
Facebook is one of the oldest and biggest social media platforms. people use it to connect with friends, groups, and communities. it is owned by meta and has about 3 billion users.
X
X, formerly known as Twitter, is used for news, discussions, and trending topics. it is especially popular for real-time updates and has around 500 million users worldwide.
