Qabeli-palaw-recipe(luxurious Afghan dish)

this is the recipe for cooking qabeli

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Meat
  • Potato
  • Carrot

Instructions

  1. wash the rice and and soak it in a warm water
  2. boil water and a pot and then cook the rice for 5-10 minutes
  3. cut the meat and slice some onions then mix it and add some salt and pepper
  4. slice the carrots and potatoes , cook the potatoes within a pot with some oil
  5. layer the rice , then meat than meat and potato , sprinke the carrots on top
  6. finally cover the pot and steam it for 20-30 minutes .
