The Wisdom of Prophet Muhammad(PBUH)

knwoledge is the foundation of a meaningful life. As Prophet Muhammmad(PBUH) said, Seek knwoledge from the cradle to the grave.

On knwoledge

Seeking knwoledge is an obligation upon every Muslim. knwoledge is the life of the mind and the lamp of the soul. A person who seeks knwoledge for the sake of Allah will be elevated in this world and the next.

—Prophet Muhammad, Sahih Bukhari

On Kindness

The best among you are those who have the best manners and character.

Show kindness to others, for kindness is a sign of true faith.

A kind word is a form of charity that costs nothing but means everything to those who recive it.

—Prophet Muhammad(PBUH), Sahih Muslim

On Faith

None of you truly belives until he loves for his brother what he loves for himself.

Faith is not merely words spoken by the tongue, but a conviction held deep in the heart.

Every action you take is a reflection of the faith you carry within you.

—Prophet Muhammmad, Sunnah Ibn Majah
